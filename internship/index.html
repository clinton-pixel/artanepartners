Artane Partners internship enquiries

Artane Partners does not operate an internship programme. If you received an internship offer using the Artane Partners name - including any investment banking internship agreement - it did not come from the firm, and you should treat it as attempted fraud.

The position

  • The firm has no internship or unpaid placement programme, and has never offered one.
  • The firm does not recruit through mass direct messages, and does not send contracts to people who never applied through its official channels.
  • Genuine communication from the firm comes only from @artanepartners.com email addresses.

Red flags of recruiting fraud

  • Any request for a password or login. No legitimate employer asks for your LinkedIn password, email password, one-time codes or account access. Ever.
  • Any request for payment. Training fees, equipment fees, verification fees or release fees are advance-fee fraud.
  • Non-company email domains. Free webmail addresses, or lookalike domains that are not artanepartners.com.
  • Urgency and secrecy. Pressure to sign quickly, keep the offer confidential, or move the conversation to encrypted apps.
  • Documents you cannot verify. Contracts naming a real company but routing contact through unofficial channels.

How to verify an approach

  1. Check the sender domain: only @artanepartners.com is genuine.
  2. Cross-check the firm at artanepartners.com/verify - registry records take five minutes.
  3. Call the published number, +353 1 536 5982, and ask whether the approach is genuine.

What the firm never does

  • Never charges candidates or interns any fee, at any stage.
  • Never asks for passwords, logins, PINs or one-time codes.
  • Never guarantees investment returns or asks individuals to move money.

If you received a fake offer

Do not sign anything, send money, or share account access. Report the approach to the platform where it arrived and to your local fraud authority (in Ireland, An Garda Siochana). You can forward the material to the firm via the fraud and impersonation notice so it can pursue the impersonators.

Common questions

Does Artane Partners offer internships? No. Any internship offer in the firm's name is fake.

Does the firm ask for LinkedIn passwords or account access? Never. That request alone identifies the approach as fraud.

I already shared information. What now? Change the affected passwords immediately, enable two-factor authentication, notify the platform, and contact your bank if you shared financial details.
